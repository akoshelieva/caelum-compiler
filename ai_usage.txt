AI Usage
Link:https://chatgpt.com/share/6ac527bd-2294-83ed-89bf-0b289b682ec4
AI tools were used during Stage 1 development.

AI was used for:
- discussing the design of the Caelum programming language;
- choosing keywords and syntax;
- planning the project structure;
- drafting parts of the recursive-descent parser;
- designing the AST hierarchy;
- reviewing the implementation against the assignment requirements;
- generating ideas for positive and negative tests.

Example prompts:
- "Допоможи спроєктувати структуру Stage 1 compiler project..."
- "На основі лексера і граматики Caelum реалізуй рекурсивний парсер..."
- "Перевір реалізацію Caelum Stage 1 згідно з усіма вимогами завдання..."

Parts used with modifications:
- parser structure;
- AST node structure;
- test cases;
- README structure.

All generated code was reviewed and adjusted manually to match the Caelum grammar and assignment requirements.
